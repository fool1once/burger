About us

A burger house with a big table.

Don Zulueta started with one idea: a burger that is worth sharing. Every patty is pressed to order, grilled hot and stacked by hand with fresh toppings.

It is a place for after-school hangouts, family dinners and late snacks with friends. Come hungry, stay for the shakes.
